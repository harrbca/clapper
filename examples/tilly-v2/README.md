# Tilly v2

The new Tilly (`/@kit/characters/tilly3d-v2.js`): a real 2.5 t counterbalance forklift, to scale in
millimetres, with big eyes in her windscreen and a mouth on the front of her body. She drives in, says
hello, lifts her forks all the way up and brings them down, looks round the warehouse and smiles. Voiced
by Jessica. The lab (`clap still 0 1 2 3 4 --entry scenes/lab.js`) has her turnaround and faces, her face
from every side, contacts (tyres, forks), lift and tilt, and close-ups.
